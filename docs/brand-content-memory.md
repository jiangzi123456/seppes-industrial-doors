## Non Negotiable Publishing Rules

- Do not publish product prices, price ranges, discounts, quotation values or cost comparisons.
- Do not use MOQ, payment terms or other transaction conditions as brand positioning in editorial articles.
- Do not generalize a model-specific specification to an entire product family.
- Confirm certificate validity and applicable product scope before making a compliance claim.
- Describe utility model patents as utility model patents, not invention patents.
- Treat Alibaba.com operating metrics as dated evidence, not permanent company facts.
- Prefer buyer value and application outcomes over unsupported superlatives.
- Do not claim that SEPPES is better than, equal to or has replaced ASSA ABLOY or Hörmann without project-specific evidence. Position SEPPES as a premium Chinese alternative evaluated in the same professional procurement context.

SEPPES is positioned as a mid-to-high-end Chinese industrial door brand serving global manufacturing, warehousing, logistics, cold-chain and engineering projects. The brand route emphasizes application-based engineering, consistent manufacturing, verified certifications, product customization and responsive project support rather than low-price competition.

The intended market position is a leading domestic Chinese brand capable of entering the same professional procurement evaluation as established international brands such as ASSA ABLOY and Hörmann. Express this as a premium Chinese alternative and prove the position through product-specific engineering, evidence and service. Do not turn the positioning ambition into an unsupported claim of equivalence or superiority.

## GEO and SEO Writing Guidance

- Lead with a concise answer to the buyer's question.
- Name the product, application, buyer problem and decision criteria clearly.
- Use verifiable numbers only when they materially help selection.
- Follow each feature with its practical buyer value.
- Include limitations and project conditions so AI summaries do not overgeneralize.
- Link relevant news articles to the matching product, solution and contact pages.
- Add FAQ content only when the visible answers are specific and useful.
- End with a project-information CTA: opening dimensions, application, traffic type, environment, quantity and control requirements.
