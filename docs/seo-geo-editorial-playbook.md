# SEPPES SEO/GEO Editorial Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook governs future product news, industry application articles and solution-led editorial content. The goal is to earn relevant search visibility, become easy for answer engines to understand and quote, and convert qualified industrial buyers into project conversations.

## Minimum Brief

The user may provide only three inputs:

1. Primary keyword or product.
2. Application scenario or industry.
3. Intended outcome.

Infer the likely buyer, search intent and article angle from those inputs. Ask a question only when missing information would materially change the product recommendation or create an unsupported claim. Optional inputs include target market, language, buyer role, door model, proof assets and preferred CTA.

## Source Hierarchy

Use sources in this order:

1. Current product-specific technical sheet, certificate, test report, drawing or contract.
2. Current approved product page and `docs/brand-content-memory.md`.
3. Verified project photos, videos and project records.
4. Domestic website copy in `H:\2025官网文本\2025文本\` and `H:\2024官网文本\` for topic ideas, buyer language, pain points and application patterns.
5. General industry knowledge, clearly qualified where project conditions matter.

Domestic copy is an idea and terminology library, not automatic proof. Never inherit an old number, customer name, certificate claim, component brand, performance percentage or comparative claim without current evidence.

## Positioning

Present SEPPES as a mid-to-high-end Chinese industrial door brand and a premium Chinese alternative considered in professional procurement alongside established international brands. Build this position through engineering fit, manufacturing consistency, verified certification, customization, system integration and project support.

ASSA ABLOY and Hörmann may be named only when the comparison is useful to buyer intent and supported by like-for-like evidence. Do not claim that SEPPES is equal to, better than, replaces or is used instead of either brand without project-specific proof. Avoid attacking low-priced suppliers; explain lifecycle risk, configuration differences and evidence buyers should inspect.

## Required Article Architecture

Every article should form a problem-to-decision path:

1. Search-focused title: product or problem + application + buyer outcome.
2. Direct answer: answer the main question in the opening 40–80 words.
3. Scenario and pain: explain the workflow, environment and operational consequence.
4. Why the problem occurs: show the mechanism, not just symptoms.
5. Recommended door or entrance configuration: connect each feature to buyer value.
6. Selection criteria: dimensions, traffic, frequency, environment, controls and safety.
7. System boundary: state what the door helps with and what requires HVAC, extraction, fire protection or other systems.
8. SEPPES fit and proof: include only evidence approved in the brand memory.
9. Practical FAQ: answer real procurement and engineering questions.
10. Conversion CTA: request opening dimensions, quantity, application, traffic type, environment, control requirements, project location and available photos or drawings.

Do not force all ten elements into separate sections when a shorter article would answer the intent better.

## SEO Rules

- Assign one primary search intent and one primary keyword to each page.
- Use natural related terms, product synonyms, industry entities and decision criteria; never repeat a keyword mechanically.
- Keep one clear H1. Use descriptive H2/H3 headings that can stand alone in search results.
- Write a unique title and meta description aligned with the visible page.
- Link to the matching product, solution, supporting article and contact page when they genuinely help the reader.
- Use descriptive image filenames, meaningful alt text and captions that add application context.
- Add Article schema to editorial pages. Add FAQ schema only when the same useful questions and answers are visible on the page.
- Use a canonical URL and include indexable articles in the sitemap.
- Prefer improving an existing page that serves the same intent over creating a competing page.

## GEO and Answer-Engine Rules

- Put the concise answer before the long explanation.
- Use self-contained passages that name the product, application, condition and outcome.
- State units, model scope and project conditions next to quantitative claims.
- Separate verified facts, recommended configurations and examples.
- Explain limitations explicitly so summaries do not overgeneralize.
- Use short comparison tables, checklists and FAQs when they make a decision easier to extract.
- Keep brand identity and product terminology consistent across articles.
- Favor specific operational language over slogans, vague superlatives and unsupported adjectives.

## Conversion Rules

- Address the buyer's operational risk before describing the product.
- Translate every feature into a practical outcome, such as reduced open time, smoother forklift flow, improved separation, clearer safety logic or easier integration.
- Show why configuration and engineering support matter for demanding applications.
- Use proof near the claim it supports.
- End with a low-friction project-information CTA rather than a generic sales slogan.
- Do not publish prices, ranges, discounts, quotations or cost comparisons.

## Evidence Gate for Legacy Domestic Copy

The following recurring claims in the 2024–2025 domestic corpus require current source verification before publication:

- Exact opening speeds, maximum sizes, wind ratings, cycle-life figures and response times.
- Energy, efficiency, dust-reduction, accident-reduction or maintenance-savings percentages.
- Customer counts, country counts, service-outlet counts and named-customer usage.
- Insurance amounts, global warranty wording and warranty duration.
- CE, UL, SGS, TÜV, CNAS, ISO or other certificate scope and validity.
- Named component origins or brands such as motors, controllers and PVC materials.
- Claims such as zero delay, zero rework, maintenance-free, ten years without failure, exclusive, industry benchmark or first-tier ranking.

When proof is unavailable, convert the statement into a qualified buyer-value explanation or omit it.

## Default Deliverable

Unless the user asks for another format, provide:

- SEO title, meta description and suggested URL slug.
- Primary keyword, related terms and search intent.
- Complete publish-ready article.
- Suggested internal links.
- Image placement ideas with alt text.
- Visible FAQ questions and answers.
- Article/FAQ schema recommendation.
- Conversion CTA.
- A short evidence checklist identifying any claim that still needs product-specific verification.
